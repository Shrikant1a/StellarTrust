## Summary of Major Technical Improvements
- **Security & Gas Optimization**: Added reentrancy guards to the core escrow contract and optimized Gas fees for Mainnet executions (`acd81fe`, `566a44a`).
- **Multi-Asset Soroban Contract**: Integrated USDC alongside native XLM payments for stable transaction routing (`2cf871d`, `j7k8l9m`).
- **Core Features**: Launched Trust & Reputation Scoring (TRS) engine (`cbbb183`), universal Stellar Wallet Kit (`69fdd62`), print-friendly export agreements (`1941ef8`), arbitration fee estimator (`4141bc8`), and USD conversion feed (`4eee71a`).
- **Monitoring & Indexing**: Expanded monitoring with dynamic client-side Network Toggle (Testnet/Mainnet support) enabling real-time metrics switching and network RPC latency tracking (`92f6044`, `dd180dd`).
